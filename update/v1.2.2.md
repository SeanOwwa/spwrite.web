This update gives you full control over where your Word exports are saved.

### New

- **Choose where exports are saved.** Exporting to Word now opens your computer's Save As dialog, with the project name already filled in as the file name. Change the name or folder however you like — and if you remove the `.docx` ending, Spwrite adds it back for you. Changed your mind? Cancelling just closes the dialog, with nothing saved and no error.
- **It remembers your export folder.** The folder you last saved to is remembered the next time you export, so you're not hunting for it every time. The export window shows where it'll save, with a **Change…** button to pick a different spot up front. If that folder is gone, Spwrite falls back to your Downloads (or Documents) folder.
- **Show in folder.** After an export, the confirmation shows the full path of the saved file and offers a **Show in folder** button — opening Finder on macOS, Explorer on Windows, or your file manager on Linux, right at the file.
