### First public beta

The first beta release of Spwrite — a calm, distraction-free writing app that runs
entirely on your own computer.

**Highlights**

- Manuscript editor with classic serif type and a live word count
- Projects, folders, and documents to organize your work
- Drag to reorder and sort documents into folders
- Format as you write: bold, italic, headings, lists, and links
- Character panel for names, roles, details, and portraits
- Export any selection of documents to a single `.docx`
- Autosave — everything saves on your machine, fully offline

**Platforms**

- macOS (Apple Silicon & Intel)
- Windows 10 & 11

Free to use for every writer. New builds ship about every two weeks.
