A big update — Spwrite now has a private writing assistant, project-wide search
by meaning, and book cover photos, plus a refreshed look and a long list of fixes.

### New

- **A private AI writing assistant.** Open the AI Panel from the editor toolbar to brainstorm, rephrase, continue a passage, or summarize. It runs entirely on your computer — no account, no sign-in, and your writing never leaves your device.
  - It can answer from your own project, drawing on your documents and characters and showing which one it used.
  - Streaming replies with a typing indicator and a stop button.
  - Your conversations are saved per project and come back when you reopen it, and a **New chat** button clears them when you want a fresh start.
  - Works offline after a one-time model download, which shows its size and progress up front.
- **Search your whole project by meaning.** Ask a question and Spwrite finds the right passage across every chapter and book — even if you worded it differently from how you wrote it. Great for continuity checks like "what colour are Mara's eyes?"
- **Project cover photos.** Give each project a book cover when you create it or later from Edit project. Covers show on your dashboard and in the sidebar. Projects without one keep a colourful initial badge.
- **Create a project with Cmd+N / Ctrl+N** from the dashboard.
- A small version label is shown next to the Projects title so you always know which build you're on.

### Improved

- **A refreshed look across Mac, Windows, and Linux**, keeping the calm navy and dark-cyan theme — a responsive grid of portrait cover cards, clearer focus and hover feedback, roomier controls, and a comfortable reading width in the editor.
- **Word export now keeps your formatting.** Headings, bullet and numbered lists, and bold, italic, underline, and strikethrough all carry over to the `.docx` file.
- Renaming a project now happens in the Edit project dialog.

### Fixed

- **Your spacing stays put.** Blank lines between paragraphs, or one at the end of a document, no longer disappear after saving and reopening. (Documents saved before this fix may have already lost their blank lines, and those can't be recovered.)
- The AI assistant no longer fails on longer questions.
- Picking an unsupported or broken image as a cover now shows a friendly message instead of causing a problem.

### Note

- Spwrite is now a desktop app for **Mac, Windows, and Linux** (and the web). There are no phone or tablet versions.
