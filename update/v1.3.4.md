A big writing-experience update: word-processor keyboard shortcuts, a true
focus mode, session word count, and built-in guides — plus important fixes to
how your formatting and last-second edits are saved.

### New

- **Keyboard shortcuts, like a word processor** (Cmd on Mac, Ctrl on Windows and Linux): bold, italic, links, headings 1–3 and normal text, numbered and bulleted lists, redo, save now, focus mode, hide the sidebar, and a built-in shortcut list. Hover any toolbar button to see its shortcut.
- **Focus mode.** Hides the sidebar, title, toolbar, and panels — leaving just your page and a small word-count chip. Press Esc to leave.
- **Words this session.** The word count now also shows how many words you've added since you opened the document (e.g. "1,240 words · +312").
- **Em dash made easy.** Type three hyphens (`---`) and they turn into a proper "—".
- **More room at the bottom of the page**, so your last lines can scroll up to eye level instead of sitting at the very bottom.
- **Built-in User Guide.** A read-only guide project, always at the top of your dashboard, covering every feature — and it updates itself when a new version changes it.
- **Guides toggle** next to New project to show or hide the built-in guide. Your choice is remembered.

### Changed

- **The AI assistant is temporarily marked "Coming soon"** while it's being stabilised. There's no model download or background indexing in this build. Writing, editing, and everything else work as normal.

### Fixed

- **Italic and bold text now save correctly.** Previously, italic text could come back wrapped in underscores after autosave and reopening when your selection included a space or part of a word. Documents already affected are repaired automatically when you open them.
- **No more lost last-second edits.** The final seconds of typing could be lost — or saved to the wrong document — when switching documents, closing a project, or quitting within the autosave window. Pending edits are now saved first.
- **List bullets and numbers line up with the text** again, using the same font, size, and spacing instead of floating above it.
- **Removed shortcuts that added formatting which vanished on save** (underline, strikethrough, inline code, quote, checklist, indent, image), so what you see is what gets saved.
