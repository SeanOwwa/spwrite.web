Four simple steps

Follow along in order. Screenshots optional — it's quick.

1

Download the app

Click the Download Spwrite for Mac button above. Your Mac saves a file called Spwrite-macOS-v1.0.0.zip into your Downloads folder (find it in the dock, or in Finder under Downloads).

2

Unzip it

Double-click Spwrite-macOS-v1.0.0.zip. macOS unpacks it right away and leaves you with the app itself: Spwrite.app (it just shows as Spwrite with the app icon).

3

Drag it into Applications

Drag Spwrite into your Applications folder. In any Finder window, Applications is in the left sidebar — drop Spwrite there. Now it lives with your other apps and shows up in Launchpad and Spotlight, ready to open any time. That's the whole "installation."

4

Open it and start writing

Double-click Spwrite. The first time only, macOS may say it "can't be opened because it is from an unidentified developer." That's normal for an app installed this way — see the tip just below to open it in two clicks.

If macOS blocks the first open

  1. Right-click (or hold Control and click) the Spwrite app.
  2. Choose Open from the menu.
  3. In the box that appears, click Open again.
  4. You only do this once. After that, Spwrite opens with a normal double-click.

This happens because the app isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer certificate — it's safe, it just hasn't paid Apple's toll. Right-click → Open tells your Mac you trust it.

Common questions

Where is my writing saved?

Right on your Mac. Nothing goes to the cloud, and Spwrite works with no internet connection.

Do I need to install anything else?

No. This is the ready-to-run app — just download, drag, and open.

How do I remove it?

Drag the Spwrite app to the Trash. That's it.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free to use. If it helps your writing, a coffee is always appreciated.

Is this the final version?

Not yet — Spwrite is in beta. It's ready for everyday writing, and a new build ships about every two weeks. To update, just download the latest here and replace the app in Applications.
