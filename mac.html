Four simple steps

Follow along in order. Screenshots optional — it's quick.

1

Download the app

Click the Download Spwrite for Mac button above. Your Mac saves a file called Spwrite-macOS-v1.2.3.zip into your Downloads folder (find it in the dock, or in Finder under Downloads).

2

Unzip it

Double-click Spwrite-macOS-v1.2.3.zip. macOS unpacks it right away and leaves you with the app itself: Spwrite.app (it just shows as Spwrite with the app icon).

3

Copy it into Applications

Drag Spwrite into your Applications folder. In any Finder window, Applications is in the left sidebar — drop Spwrite there. Now it lives with your other apps and shows up in Launchpad and Spotlight, ready to open any time. That's the whole "installation."

Want it in your Dock for one-click access? Open it once, then right-click its Dock icon and choose Options → Keep in Dock.

4

Open it and start writing

Double-click Spwrite. The first time only, macOS says "Apple could not verify Spwrite is free of malware." That's normal. Follow the short steps just below once, and you're done.

Opening Spwrite the first time

  1. When the warning appears, click Done.
  2. Open System Settings (the gear icon in the Dock, or Apple menu → System Settings).
  3. Click Privacy & Security in the sidebar, then scroll down to the Security section.
  4. Next to "Spwrite" was blocked, click Open Anyway.
  5. Enter your Mac password (or use Touch ID), then click Open Anyway once more.

You only do this once. After that, Spwrite opens with a normal double-click, even after you update it.

Why does this happen? Spwrite is a free beta, and it isn't signed with Apple's paid ($99/year) developer certificate. The app is safe. macOS shows this message for every unsigned app, and many free and open-source Mac apps work the same way.

Common questions

Where is my writing saved?

Right on your Mac. Nothing goes to the cloud, and Spwrite works with no internet connection.

Do I need to install anything else?

No. This is the ready-to-run app — just download, drag, and open.

I don't see "Open Anyway"

Try to open Spwrite once first, so the warning appears. The button then shows in Privacy & Security for about an hour. If you're comfortable with Terminal, you can also run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Spwrite.app and then double-click Spwrite.

How do I remove it?

Drag the Spwrite app to the Trash. That's it.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free to use. If it helps your writing, a coffee is always appreciated.

Is this the final version?

Not yet — Spwrite is in beta. It's ready for everyday writing, and a new build ships about every two weeks. To update, just download the latest here and replace the app in Applications.
