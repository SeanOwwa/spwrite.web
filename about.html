Spwrite is a quiet place to write — no accounts, no cloud, no subscription. Here's why it exists, who builds it, and the promise that it stays free.

Why we made Spwrite

Most writing apps want your login, your files in their cloud, and a monthly fee for the privilege. We wanted the opposite.

🕯️

A calm place to write

A deep-navy workspace, a classic serif manuscript, and a live word count. Everything else gets out of the way so the words can come.

🔒

Your words stay yours

Everything saves on your own machine and works fully offline. No account to make, nothing uploaded, nothing tracked.

🧭

Simple on purpose

Projects, folders, cover photos, focus mode, characters, and Word export — the features a writer actually reaches for, and not much else. A private AI assistant is on the way.

Who develops it

A small, independent project — not a company chasing growth.

Built by Seanless

Spwrite is created and directed by Seanless, with a hand from Kiro along the way. It's an independent project built for the love of writing — every decision is made with writers in mind, not investors or ads.

Still in beta, actively growing

Spwrite is a beta release, not a finished 1.0 — it works well for daily writing, but it's still evolving. A fresh build ships every two weeks with new features, refinements, and fixes, so the app keeps getting better the whole time you use it.

Why it's free

We believe the tool you write in shouldn't cost you a subscription. Writing is hard enough.

Our promise to writers

  1. Spwrite is free to use for every writer — hobbyist, student, or professional.
  2. No subscription, no paywall, no locked features — the whole app is yours to use.
  3. As long as Seanless is the director, Spwrite stays free to use. That's the commitment behind this project.

If Spwrite helps your writing and you'd like to give back, a coffee is always appreciated — but it's never required, and it never unlocks anything. Everything is free for everyone.
