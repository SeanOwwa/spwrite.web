Three simple steps

Follow along in order. Screenshots optional — it's quick.

1

Download the app

Click the Download Spwrite for Windows button above. Your browser saves a file called Spwrite-v1.0.0.exe into your Downloads folder (open it from the browser's downloads bar, or in File Explorer under Downloads).

2

Run it

Double-click Spwrite-v1.0.0.exe. That's the whole "installation" — Spwrite is a single self-contained app, so there's no setup wizard. The first time only, Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box — see the tip just below to get past it in two clicks.

3

Start writing

Spwrite opens and you're ready to go. Want it handy later? Right-click Spwrite-v1.0.0.exe and choose Pin to Start or Pin to taskbar, or drag it into a folder like Documents so it's easy to find.

If Windows blocks the first open

  1. When the blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears, click More info.
  2. A new button appears at the bottom — click Run anyway.
  3. Spwrite opens. You only do this once; after that it runs with a normal double-click.

This happens because the app isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate — it's safe, it just hasn't paid the toll yet. More info → Run anyway tells Windows you trust it.

Common questions

Where is my writing saved?

Right on your PC. Nothing goes to the cloud, and Spwrite works with no internet connection.

Do I need to install anything else?

No. This is the ready-to-run app — just download and double-click.

How do I remove it?

Delete Spwrite-v1.0.0.exe. That's it — there's nothing else to uninstall.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free to use. If it helps your writing, a coffee is always appreciated.

Is this the final version?

Not yet — Spwrite is in beta. It's ready for everyday writing, and a new build ships about every two weeks. To update, just download the latest here and delete the older Spwrite .exe.
