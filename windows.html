Four simple steps

Built for Windows 11 on ARM64. Follow along in order — it's quick.

1

Download the app

Click the Download Spwrite for Windows button above. Your browser saves a file called Spwrite-Windows-v1.2.3.zip into your Downloads folder (open it from the browser's downloads bar, or in File Explorer under Downloads).

2

Unzip it

Right-click Spwrite-Windows-v1.2.3.zip and choose Extract All…, then Extract. You'll get a Spwrite folder containing spwrite.exe and the files it needs to run. Keep everything in that folder together — move the whole folder, never just the .exe on its own.

3

Open the app

Open the Spwrite folder and double-click spwrite.exe. The first time only, Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box — see the tip just below to get past it in two clicks.

4

Keep it handy

Move the Spwrite folder somewhere permanent (like Documents), then right-click spwrite.exe and choose Pin to Start or Create shortcut for your Desktop. Tip: avoid running it straight from a OneDrive-synced Desktop, which can stop it starting.

If Windows blocks the first open

  1. When the blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears after opening spwrite.exe, click More info.
  2. A new button appears at the bottom — click Run anyway.
  3. Spwrite opens. You only do this once; after that it runs with a normal double-click.

This happens because the app isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate — it's safe, it just hasn't paid the toll yet. More info → Run anyway tells Windows you trust it.

Common questions

Where is my writing saved?

Right on your PC. Nothing goes to the cloud, and Spwrite works with no internet connection.

Do I need to install anything else?

No. The Spwrite folder includes everything it needs, including the Microsoft Visual C++ runtime, so it runs on a fresh PC without extra downloads.

How do I remove it?

Delete the Spwrite folder (and any shortcut you made). That's it — there's nothing else to uninstall.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free to use. If it helps your writing, a coffee is always appreciated.

Is this the final version?

Not yet — Spwrite is in beta. It's ready for everyday writing, and a new build ships about every two weeks. To update, download the latest here and replace your old Spwrite folder.
