A calm place

Spwrite is a distraction-free writing app for your own computer. Organize your work into projects, folders, and documents, and write in a clean manuscript editor. Everything is saved on your machine — always with you, always offline.

Get Spwrite

What it looks like

Designed to stay out of your way for long writing sessions.

📖

Manuscript editor

Classic serif type, double-spaced — the same standard as a Google Docs manuscript, easy on the eyes and easy to mark up.

🔢

Live word count

A running word count sits in the upper-right of the document and updates as you type.

🌌

Deep navy theme

A dark navy workspace with dark-cyan highlights and a soft gradient backdrop, gentle for late-night drafts.

✨

Tactile interface

Rounded cards with subtle depth, project tiles that lift on hover with colorful initial badges, and pill-shaped controls throughout.

What you can do

🗂️

Projects & folders

Keep separate projects for different books, articles, or clients, and sort each into folders and documents.

✋

Drag to organize

Grab the handle on any row to reorder. Drop a document into a folder, back out to the top level, or between folders.

🅱️

Format as you write

Bold, italic, headings, lists, and links. Tab drops a clean 10-space indent right where your cursor is.

📄

Export to Word

Send any selection of documents to a single .docx — each title a heading, each document starting on a new page.

🎭

Character panel

Keep your cast close: name, role, free-form details, and a portrait for each character, right beside your draft.

💾

Autosave

No Save button to remember. A status pill shows "Saving…" as you type, then "Saved" once your text is safely on disk.
