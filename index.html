A calm place
for long work.

Spwrite is a calm writing app for long work — novels, scripts, essays, and notes. It runs on your own computer, works offline, and keeps your writing private. There's no account and nothing to pay.

Get Spwrite — it's free See what's inside

Write comfortably

A workspace that stays quiet and out of your way, so the words can come.

📖

Manuscript-style page

Book serif type, double line spacing, and a comfortable line width — easy on the eyes for long sessions.

🧘

Focus mode

Hide everything except the page — sidebar, title, toolbar, and panels all step aside. Press Esc to come back.

🔢

Word count & session count

See your total word count, plus how many words you've added since you opened the document.

✍️

Small touches

Formatting for bold, italic, headings, lists, and links. Type --- for an em dash (—), and press Tab to indent a paragraph.

Organize your work

Keep novels, scripts, essays, and notes in order — however your mind works.

🖼️

Projects as book covers

A project for each book, article, or client, shown as cover cards. Add a cover photo, or keep a colorful initial badge.

🗂️

Folders & documents

Inside each project, group parts, chapters, and scenes into folders and documents.

✋

Drag to organize

Drag to reorder. Drop a document onto a folder to move it in, or back out to the top level. Rename/delete stay hidden until you long-press a row.

💾

Never lose a word

Autosave kicks in a couple of seconds after you stop. A chip shows "Saving…" then "Saved" — and switching, closing, or quitting saves first.

🎭

Keep your cast close

The Characters panel holds each character's name, role, details, and picture, right beside your draft.

📄

Export to Word

Pick the documents you want and send them to a single .docx — each one starts on a new page with its title as a heading.

📘

Built-in guides

A read-only User Guide walks through every feature, right inside the app. Toggle the guides off any time to hide them.

Coming soon

A private AI assistant, in the works

We're building a writing assistant that runs on your own computer — no account, no cloud, nothing sent anywhere. It's being stabilised and isn't part of this build yet, so it shows as "Coming soon" in the app and makes no network requests. Everything else works fully offline today.
