Where the

Spwrite is a calm, distraction-free writing app for your own computer. Organize books, articles, and clients into projects, write in a clean manuscript editor, and lean on a private AI that actually knows your story. Everything saves on your machine — always with you, always offline.

Get Spwrite — it's free See what's inside

Made for long writing sessions

A workspace that stays quiet and out of your way, so the words can come.

📖

Manuscript editor

Classic serif type, double-spaced — the same standard as a Google Docs manuscript. Easy on the eyes and easy to mark up.

🔢

Live word count

A running word count sits in the upper-right of the document and updates as you type — progress you can feel.

🌌

Deep navy theme

A dark navy workspace with dark-cyan highlights and a soft gradient backdrop, gentle for late-night drafts.

🖼️

Book-cover dashboard

Your projects sit in a responsive grid of portrait cover cards. Give each book a cover, or keep a colorful initial badge.

Everything a writer reaches for

The tools that matter, and not much else.

🗂️

Projects & folders

Keep separate projects for different books, articles, or clients, and sort each into folders and documents.

✋

Drag to organize

Grab the handle on any row to reorder. Drop a document into a folder, back out to the top level, or between folders.

🅱️

Format as you write

Bold, italic, headings, lists, and links. Tab drops a clean 10-space indent right where your cursor is, and your blank lines stay exactly as you left them.

📄

Export to Word

Send any selection of documents to a single .docx — titles become headings, formatting carries over, and each document starts on a new page.

🎭

Character panel

Keep your cast close: name, role, free-form details, and a portrait for each character, right beside your draft.

💾

Autosave

No Save button to remember. A status pill shows "Saving…" as you type, then "Saved" once your text is safely on disk.

A writing assistant that stays private

Spwrite's AI runs a small language model on your own computer. No account, no API key, no telemetry, no per-use cost — and your writing never leaves your device.

🔒

Private by design

Chat about your draft — brainstorm, rephrase, continue a passage, or summarize. Nothing you write is ever sent anywhere.

📚

Knows your story

Ask about your own project and the assistant answers from your documents and characters, showing which source it drew from.

🔎

Semantic search

Search a whole manuscript by meaning, not just matching words — it finds the right passage across every chapter and book.

🛜

Offline after setup

A one-time model download, verified for integrity, then it works with no connection at all. The app stays usable while it downloads.

Powered by openly licensed on-device models (Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct and bge-small-en-v1.5, both Apache-2.0). Web research and AI image generation are marked Coming soon and make no network requests today.
